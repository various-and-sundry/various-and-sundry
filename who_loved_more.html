Who Loved More?

The Younger Man

He lay asleep in his bed. He would need rest in order to rise for work the next day. Suddenly, he was awoken by a sound. The sound came from his phone. He roused himself to see who would call at such a strange hour. To his surprise, the caller ID showed it was the one whom he loved. They had been dating for over a year now. He hoped someday she would be his wife. Their relationship was close and dear and loving.

Confused and a little worried, he picked up the phone. He was met with an apologetic voice on the other end, clearly conscious that she was disturbing him at an hour when he should be asleep. She apologized for waking him, but she needed help. Miles from home, she was stuck on the side of the road. Her tire had gone flat, and she didn't know what to do.

With resolution in his voice, and without hesitation, he said he was coming. He sprang from his bed and readied himself. He knew what he had to do. He was invigorated. Any sense of drowsiness that hung over his eyes when he had answered the phone was gone. As he jumped behind the wheel and turned the key, he was filled with something that could only be described as anticipation and excitement. He was a man given the chance to rescue his damsel in distress.

He could never admit that he would wish for something like this to happen, but deep down, a part of him was jubilant at the opportunity to show his self-sacrificing love. As he drove, he rehearsed over and over in his head what he might say and do. He considered exactly how he might call her name in an anxious tone, and perhaps how he might then ask her if she was alright. He also pondered what she might say back to him, and how thankful and doting she might be. The thought put butterflies in his stomach.

When he finally pulled over, her adoring thankfulness was as wonderful as anything he had pictured. Her embrace and profuse gratitude filled his heart with joy. Her guilt and embarrassment over being such a burden in the middle of the night had brought her to tears. He comforted her and told her she should never ever think twice about calling him in times like this.

He prepared the jack and began the work. As the tire was replaced, she continued telling him how strong and smart he was, and how she would never be able to change the tire herself. He responded, of course, by telling her that she was very smart, and that she could have changed the tire herself if she put her mind to it. Nevertheless, deep down, he reveled in her declaration of her utter helplessness without him.

Upon finishing the job, he put his jack away, and she embraced him again and thanked him for being such a wonderful man. He, of course, told her not to mention it, but deep down, he loved the mentioning of it. He reveled in every compliment, in every adoring statement, in every hug, in the looks of love that she gave him.

He could have gone home, but he told her that he wanted to make absolutely sure that the fix was okay. He offered to follow her home in his car to make sure that she got there and had no further trouble. She, of course, thanked him further for this. He got into his car, she got into hers, and they headed towards her house.

As they drove along, his mind worked hard, trying to recall each and every compliment and declaration of love. He did his best to remember them word for word, and then he picked out favorites from among them. He also thought of what he might say when they arrived at her house, and what she might say, and whether he might receive another hug and a few more doting comments to turn over in his mind throughout the coming weeks.

He also thought of what he might say to her, how he would tell her that she should never hesitate to call him in times like this, and that he would have driven much farther and done much more. He wanted her to know that he would do anything for her.

When they arrived at her house, there was a last round of gratitude that would sound redundant to anyone not in the throes of love. Then, they parted ways, and he returned home to catch a little sleep before the next day.

However, he didn't sleep. In his mind, he couldn't stop replaying what she had said. The next day, he was very groggy at work, but it was a grogginess filled with adrenaline. He derived so much joy from what had happened. His expression of love through his actions, and her expressions of love through her words played out continually in his mind.

The man never forgot this episode, and for years, it was one of his favorite memories.

The Older Man

That same night, a much older man lay asleep in his bed. He hoped to retire soon, but his days of work were not over. He needed rest in order to rise for work the next day.

However, his slumber was interrupted by the sound of his phone. He roused himself and saw that his wife was calling him. They had been married for 40 years, and they had never once been unfaithful to one another. He, of course, answered the call. He wouldn't think of doing otherwise.

His wife was on the side of the road. Her tire had been punctured, and she needed her husband's help. He felt no wave of adrenaline or excitement, but he knew what he had to do. His wife needed him to change that tire, and it never even occurred to him that he could do anything other than get up and do just that.

He rose from bed, prepared himself, and headed out to meet his wife on the side of the road. As he drove, he tried to remember how old that tire was. He also thought about how he would likely be tired the next day at work.

When he arrived, he asked his wife about what had happened and which tire needed to be changed. She provided the information, and he got to work.

She apologized for the major inconvenience, and he said it was fine. They didn't talk much during his work. She didn't want to distract him. They had lived together for 40 years, and there sometimes wasn't much to talk about.

Once the spare tire was in place and the jack was put away, she thanked him again and gave one more brief apology. He said not to worry about it. They got into their vehicles and headed home.

At home, they went to bed with very few words. They wanted to get some sleep before the next day. The man slept fairly well without much on his mind.

He was quite tired when the alarm woke him up, but he had no choice but to rise and to head to work. He thought about what had happened, but only because he needed to schedule the time to replace the spare tire with a full-size one. He also thought about whether to simply put all four winter tires on now. Once that was done, he hardly ever thought of that night again.
